The divider is the link.

Three stacked hairlines at 1 / 2 / 1 px — control, bulk, video. A packet rides the bulk lane once when the rail scrolls into view. It replaces every <hr> on the site.

With JavaScript off the rail is still a rail; the packet simply never fires. Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, lanes.js does not arm at all.

One tag per claim, colour-coded by strength.

Hover or focus any tag for its definition. The same vocabulary appears inline in prose and in every matrix cell, so a reader learns it once.
